# Rutz's PF2e Settlements

[Discord](https://discord.gg/bB7Ca666Bv) · [Wiki](https://github.com/Rutz179/rutz-pf2e-settlements/wiki)

Villages, towns, cities and metropolises by GM Core. A settlement's level says what is for sale, the highest Earn Income task, how far spellcasting and teachers go.

- **Settlements** — a stat block, GM Core's settlement abilities, and a focus (commerce, religion, port, frontier …) that fills in abilities, traditions and teachers.
- **Reputation** — 0–100 with GM Core's bands; the GM moves it with a reason.
- **Work & training** — Earn Income jobs with DC and pay by rank; training that characters start and finish over days.
- **Shops & specialties** — with Rutz's PF2e Shop, shops stocked to the settlement's level; items the settlement is known for.
- Mark where the party is, show or hide a settlement from players, export it to a journal.

## Install
Foundry → *Add-on Modules* → *Install Module* → Manifest URL:
```
https://github.com/Rutz179/rutz-pf2e-settlements/releases/latest/download/module.json
```
Foundry VTT v13–v14 · PF2e · English / 한국어

The Settlements button in the Rutz toolkit group of the scene controls, or `game.rutzSettlements.open()`. Works with Rutz's PF2e Days (Earn Income and training follow where the party is).
